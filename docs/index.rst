Synchrotron Motion Simulator
============================

The synchrotron motion simulator is designed to simulate the longitudinal motion
for particles under RF (Radio Frequency) voltage field in a ring accelerator.
It is especially used to study the efficiency of adiabatic capture for a booster
ring. The purpose of adiabatic capture is to optimize RF voltage settings during
the ramping of beam energy and obtain the greatest efficiency of particle capture.

This documentation presents the physics and mathematical equations behind
the synchrotron motion simulation, based on the study of longitudinal
(synchrotron) motion for particles in the TLS (Taiwan Light Source) booster.
